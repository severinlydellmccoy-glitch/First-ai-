All three failed with "host not found", which means this session's network blocks them. The Scout couldn't open Amazon either, and the Prospector couldn't open Fiverr, Etsy or YouTube. The Prospector's report also says that, under Etsy's October 2026 rules, listings need at least one unedited photo of the real item. That comes from seller blogs, not Etsy itself. If it's true, we'd have to order and photograph samples first, which could push us past the Christmas shipping cut-off.
3. **The service ideas are a new direction, and the owner must agree to it.** The business plan is merchandise on Etsy and Amazon. Ideas 3 to 5 need a Fiverr or Upwork seller account, and only the owner can open accounts. The Boss can't approve that alone.
4. **The Fiverr prices are probably search-filter ranges, not real prices.** "Up to $50" and "$50-$100" look like Fiverr's price-filter labels. The profit votes assume we'd earn $50 per order, but real prices could be much lower (the report itself mentions $5 to $18 gigs for similar work).

6. **Fee rules are second-hand.** Etsy's fees, Fiverr's 20% cut and the Amazon Merch royalty change all come from blogs, not the platforms. Check them in the seller dashboards before setting prices.
