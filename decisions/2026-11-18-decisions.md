## 1. Make next

### Pick 1: Funny holiday cat sweatshirt (and t-shirt), our own cat character. Quick win.

- **Why:** It is the best fit for the business plan: our own original character on a sweatshirt, sold on Etsy for Christmas. All three judges approved it. Profit is about **$11 per sweatshirt at $34.99**, or about **$6** if Etsy's offsite ad fee applies (I re-checked the sum: $34.99 minus about $15.24 base cost, $4.75 shipping and $3.77 Etsy fees). Evidence is **medium-weak**: the strongest number (900+ bought a month) is for a mug, and the sweatshirt signal is only "100+ a month" on two Amazon listings with unknown dates.
- **Rules for the design:** Our own cartoon cat and our own jokes only (for example, "supervising" the tree, tangled in lights, sitting in the gift box). Never use the words "Grumpy Cat", and never copy another seller's slogan.
- **Products:** Crewneck sweatshirt (main) and t-shirt. **No mug** for now: it only makes money at $22 or more, and that price hasn't been tested.
- **Who works on it next:** The **Artist**. Make 2 or 3 design options in `art/drafts/` (for example `holiday-cat-tree-v1.png`), then move the chosen one to `art/final/`. The Artist robot isn't set up yet (it shows as "planned" on the station map), so until it is, this is done in a normal Claude session following the same rules.
- **Deadline:** Designs should be finished by **2026-11-24**, so there is time to list before Black Friday (November 27) and still ship before Christmas.

### Pick 2: Cozy book lover sweatshirt (sweatshirt only). Quick win.

- **Why:** This has the **best evidence of the two merch ideas** (several Amazon book-lover listings at 300 to 700+ bought a month, dates unknown). All three judges approved it. The sweatshirt has the same profit as Pick 1: about $11, or $6 with the offsite ad fee.
- **Tote is not included.** The Underboss sent the tote back: its real profit is about $4 to $7, or as little as $0.50 to $3 with Etsy's ad fee, not the $6 to $11 the Profit judge quoted. I can't pick it until the Profit judge votes again on the real Printify cost.
- **Rules for the design:** Generic reading pictures and our own wording only. No book titles, authors, publisher logos or other sellers' phrases.
- **Ask the Artist for one winter design, not only a Christmas one** (for example, a reader under a blanket with cocoa, without Christmas trees). It can keep selling after December 25, so the work isn't wasted if the Christmas window is missed.
- **Who works on it next:** The **Artist** (same note as Pick 1 about the Artist not being set up yet). Same deadline: **2026-11-24**.

### Pick 3: Listing copy for our own two products. Small, and it sets up a bigger bet.

- **Why:** This is the Prospector's best-evidenced service idea (moderate evidence, one competitor with 302 ratings). We need this work anyway: Picks 1 and 2 can't be listed without a title, Etsy's 13 tags and a description. It costs nothing and needs no new account. Doing it well on our own listings is the quality test the Underboss asked for. If our listings sell, that is the proof we'd need before ever offering this as a paid service.
- **What this pick is not:** It is **not** approval to sell listing copy on Fiverr or Upwork. That needs an account the owner would have to open (see "Needs the owner").
- **Rules:** No other brands' names in titles or tags (for example, no "Grumpy Cat"), no false claims, and say clearly that the design is our own digital art printed on demand. If Etsy asks how the item was made, answer honestly, including any use of AI.
- **Who works on it next:** The **Producer** (writes listing text). The Producer isn't set up yet either, so for now a normal Claude session writes it. Save drafts in `media/drafts/` (for example `holiday-cat-listing-etsy.md`) and move the approved version to `media/final/`. Start once the Artist's final designs exist.

## 3. Needs the owner

These involve money, accounts, publishing or settings, so they are your call. My recommendation comes first in each.

1. **Finish Printify, and stay on the free plan for now.** Printify Premium lowers the sweatshirt cost from about $15.24 to $11.22, but it is a monthly fee. Only worth it once we're selling regularly. When the account is ready, please write the service name in the "Still to decide" section of `CLAUDE.md` (never a password).
2. **Finish the Etsy shop first. Amazon Merch on Demand can wait.** Amazon's royalty on a $19.99 t-shirt reportedly fell to about $2.44 in June 2026 (from blogs, not Amazon). Keep the Amazon application going, but launch on Etsy. When the shop is open, check two things in the Etsy seller dashboard, because our figures come from blogs:
   1. The fees (listing $0.20, transaction 6.5%, payment processing 3% plus $0.25).
   2. Whether "Offsite Ads" applies to the shop, and at what rate.
3. **Check Etsy's photo rule, then decide on samples.** A seller blog says that since October 2026 Etsy listings need at least one unedited photo of the real item. If Etsy's own policy confirms this, I recommend **ordering one sample of each chosen sweatshirt** (roughly $20 each including shipping, based on the cost estimates above) as soon as the designs are final. If it's not required, list with Printify mockups and skip the samples. Either way, check Printify's published holiday shipping cut-off: if we can't list by about **December 1**, launch the winter book-lover design only and save the Christmas cat for next year.
4. **Approve each listing before it goes live.** Claude prepares the designs and listing copy. You (or a Seller robot you set up later) press publish.
5. **Fiverr or Upwork: I recommend not opening an account yet.** The business plan is merchandise first, and the setup for that isn't finished. Revisit in January, once our own listings show the copy and art are good enough to sell.
6. **Network settings: I recommend allowing amazon.com, etsy.com, fiverr.com and youtube.com** in the session's allowed sites. Every report this week was built on search snippets because those sites were blocked. Allowing them would make next week's evidence much stronger.
