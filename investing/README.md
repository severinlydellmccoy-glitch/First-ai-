# Investing

The Market Watcher's reports. Information only: the owner makes every investment decision.

- `daily/`: the market-day morning reports (`YYYY-MM-DD-report.md`) and the raw screens.
- `weekly/`: the Sunday-night week-ahead reports.

Tests set by the owner: companies over $50B; volume at least 1.5× the 20-day average; RSI rising and between 50 and 70; price above the 50-day average, and the 50-day above the 200-day.
