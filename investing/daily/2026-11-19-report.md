## What the terms mean

- **Volume** is how many shares changed hands; "3×" means three times a normal day, which usually means news. **RSI** (0 to 100) measures how strongly the price has been rising or falling over 14 days; 50 to 70 is a steady rise, and above 70 is a very fast one.
- **Moving averages** are the average closing price over the last 50 or 200 days; a price above the 50-day, with the 50-day above the 200-day, means the price has been trending up for months.
