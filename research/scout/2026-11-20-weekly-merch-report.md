# Weekly merch report: 2026-11-20

**Question this report answers:** What merchandise sold best on Amazon, Etsy and Temu in the last 30 days, what is trending on TikTok and Instagram, and what could LiquidLav make from it before Christmas?

**Short answer:** Three ideas passed all three judges. Two of them are designs we already sell, and the new numbers back them up: **funny holiday cats** (Amazon cat Christmas sweatshirts showing 800+ and 600+ "bought in past month") and **cozy bookish Christmas** (300+). The one **new** idea is a **dog-breed Christmas sweatshirt and hoodie, starting with the dachshund**. An Amazon dachshund Christmas sweatshirt shows 900+ bought in the past month, and Etsy has many competing listings. Following the Boss's rule, every idea is for the **crewneck sweatshirt plus the hoodie** only.

**How good the data is:** Still **thin**. Amazon and the trend blogs couldn't be opened directly, so everything comes from search-result summaries, and the dates of the Amazon snapshots aren't known. Etsy and Temu showed no badges or sold counts. Searches limited to TikTok and Instagram returned no posts at all. Treat this as direction, not proof.

**Timing:** Black Friday is in 7 days (Nov 27) and Cyber Monday is Nov 30. A new design needs art, a Printify draft and the owner's OK. My estimate, not a Printify figure: list the dog design by **about Dec 1**. After about Dec 10, a print-on-demand order probably won't arrive by Christmas.
