# Post-Christmas check: 2026-11-25

A short check asked for by the Analyst's dispatch (`decisions/2026-11-25-dispatch.md`). It looks past Christmas, because a new Christmas design couldn't be listed before the cut-off. The question: what's selling now in everyday winter and January themes (books, cats, dogs), and is there any early Valentine's Day demand yet? Only listings named with their own signal count. All searches were run on 2026-11-25 (US Central).

**Short answer:**

- **Everyday cats and books are still selling outside Christmas, so no new art is needed for January.** Named Amazon listings with no Christmas words show 100+ (cat) and 50+ a month, or 100+ in a week (books). The winter cat (drafts due Dec 4) and the two cozy book designs already cover this. The judges approved both themes for January.
- **There's no measurable Valentine's Day demand yet.** Plenty of cat and book Valentine sweatshirts are listed on Amazon and Etsy, and many went up in January 2026, but none showed a purchase count this week. All three Valentine ideas were denied on demand, for now. **Check again around Dec 28 to Jan 2**, when last year's listings start getting badges again. That still leaves time to make art before mid-January.
- **Etsy's Valentine cat listings sell at about $6 to $12 on sale.** Ours would be $37.67 and up, so a Valentine design would have to sell on its drawing, not its price.
- **Printify Choice's Christmas US Standard date is still unconfirmed.** printify.com couldn't be opened (the address doesn't resolve from here), and no search result gave a Printify-published date. Keep using **Dec 8**.

**How good the data is:** Thin, as before. Amazon and Etsy pages weren't opened; everything comes from search summaries with unknown snapshot dates. Temu, TikTok and Instagram weren't searched in this short run.
