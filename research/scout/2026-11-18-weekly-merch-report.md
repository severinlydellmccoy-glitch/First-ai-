## 1. Approved ideas (all three judges said yes)

### Idea 1: Funny holiday cat (original cat character)

- **The theme:** Our own cartoon cat causing small Christmas chaos, for example "supervising" the tree, tangled in lights, or sitting in the gift box instead of the gift. Use our own wording and art only. Don't use the phrases "Grumpy Cat" (a registered brand) or copy other sellers' slogans.
- **Products:** Crewneck sweatshirt (main product) and t-shirt. A mug only if it can be priced at $22 or more (see the profit note under the denied mug idea).
- **Why (the signals):**
  - Amazon listings found through search showed a cat Christmas mug with "900+ bought in past month", and two cat Christmas sweatshirts with "100+ bought in past month" each. [Amazon search, cat mugs](https://www.amazon.com/christmas-cat-mugs/s?k=christmas+cat+mugs); [Amazon, cat Christmas sweatshirt](https://www.amazon.com/Christmas-Sweatshirt-Festive-Colorful-Crewneck/dp/B0HRFFRVZM). The dates of these snapshots are unknown.
  - Etsy search results showed a "Holiday Cats" Christmas mug with about 8,200 reviews ([Etsy, holiday cat mug](https://www.etsy.com/market/holiday_cat_mug)). That is a total across all time, not the last 30 days.
  - A November 2026 print-on-demand trend article lists "funny Christmas animals" as selling now ([Playground blog](https://playgroundai.com/blog/november-2026-print-on-demand-design-trends), about 20 days old).
  - Temu has many Christmas cat sweatshirts listed ([Temu listing](https://www.temu.com/womens----christmas-sweatshirt-------christmas-cat--for--g-601099665693645.html)), but no sold counts were visible.
- **How strong the evidence is:** **Medium.** Several sources agree, and the Amazon counts come from Amazon's own "bought in past month" badges. But the dates of those snapshots can't be confirmed.
- **Votes:**
  - Demand: **approve**. Amazon purchase badges up to 900+ a month, an Etsy listing with 8,200 reviews and a recent trend article all point the same way.
  - Safety: **approve**. A cat character we draw ourselves, with our own jokes, uses no brand, character or lyric, as long as we avoid "Grumpy Cat" and copied slogans.
  - Profit: **approve**. At $34.99 on Etsy, a sweatshirt leaves about $11 profit, or about $6 if Etsy's offsite ad fee applies (see the calculation below).

- **Why (the signals):**

Again, these are all-time totals.
  - Several 2026 gift guides for readers exist ([Everyday Reading 2026 guide](https://everyday-reading.com/gift-ideas-for-readers/); [BMWinsights 2026](https://bmwinsights.com/book-christmas-gift-ideas/)). This only shows interest, not sales.
