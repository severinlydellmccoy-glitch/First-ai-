Judges: 3 approved, 8 denied

# Prospecting report, 2026-11-18

**Bottom line:** Three of eleven ideas passed all three judges. All three lean on what Claude does well: writing, plus clean layouts built from shapes and lettering.

1. Etsy and Amazon listing copy (titles, tags, descriptions) for other sellers
2. Monthly social media content packs for small businesses (captions, a posting calendar and simple branded graphics)
3. YouTube channel art (banner plus profile logo)

The owner's headline examples, YouTube thumbnails and custom pet portraits, have the strongest demand of anything I looked at. Both were **denied on profit**. Claude can't make photo-style or painted images by itself, and both markets are crowded with sellers charging $5 to $50. They could pass in a later round if a small test shows that Claude, with an image tool, can produce work as good as those sellers' (see "Next steps").

**How strong is the evidence?** Moderate on marketplace prices, weak on what people say on YouTube and Instagram. I couldn't open YouTube, Fiverr or Etsy pages in this session, and Instagram search results never showed real posts. Most numbers below come from search-result snapshots of Fiverr and Upwork listings, all seen on 2026-11-18. Check them live before setting prices.

**A note on what Claude can and can't make.** Claude writes text and can look at photos. It can also draw designs as vector files: shapes, lettering and simple illustrations, which can be saved as images. It cannot create realistic photos or painted portraits on its own. Ideas that depend on those images need a separate image tool and a quality test first.

## 1. Approved ideas

### 1. Etsy and Amazon listing copy for other sellers

- **What it is:** For each product, Claude writes a search-friendly title, Etsy's 13 tags and a description. Delivered as a document the seller can copy and paste.
- **Who buys it:** Small Etsy, Amazon and Shopify sellers who aren't confident writers or don't know how marketplace search works.
- **Where to sell it:** Fiverr and Upwork. Etsy is a marketplace for items, so it isn't the place to sell a writing service.
- **Typical prices seen:** Fiverr gigs listed "up to $50", "$50-$100" (2-day delivery) and "$10 per hour". One seller showed 302 ratings. ([bluebirby](https://fiverr.com/bluebirby/write-etsy-product-descriptions-with-seo-keywords), [sakib3102](https://fiverr.com/sakib3102/write-compelling-etsy-product-descriptions-with-seo-title), [nata6ika](https://fiverr.com/nata6ika/rewrite-your-etsy-title-description-tags-for-etsy-seo), [digimarket9](https://fiverr.com/digimarket9/do-etsy-title-description-and-tags-optimization-keyword-research-expert))
- **How Claude helps:** Claude does nearly all of it: reading the product details, suggesting keywords, and writing the title, tags and description. A person checks the result and sends it. This is the same work this business needs for its own Etsy and Amazon listings, so practising it helps both.
- **Votes:**
  - **Demand: approve.** Many competing gigs exist and at least one seller has hundreds of ratings, so people do pay for this. The evidence is moderate, not overwhelming.
  - **Safety: approve.** The work is original writing, and Fiverr allows AI in every category as long as the work isn't "generic, unmodified" AI output ([Fiverr AI guidelines](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593-Using-AI-on-Fiverr-Guidelines-for-freelancers-and-clients)). The rule for this service is no other brands' names in tags and no false claims about the product.
  - **Profit: approve.** Claude can draft a listing in minutes. After Fiverr's 20% cut ([Fiverr fees](https://vaultleap.com/blog/fiverr-fees-explained-2026)), a $50 order (a price seen in live gigs) leaves $40, minus a withdrawal fee of $0 to $3. Nothing physical has to be made or shipped.

### 2. Monthly social media content packs for small businesses

- **What it is:** A set of posts for one month, for example 12 to 20. Each post has a caption and hashtags, and the pack includes a posting calendar and simple graphics in the business's colours (quote cards, offers, opening hours, tips).
- **Who buys it:** Local shops, salons, cafes, coaches and trades. Surveys say they feel pressure to post but have little time:
  - Adobe Express (2025): 44% of owners feel pressure to post more ([Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/how-often-should-you-post-content)).
  - Markel Direct (UK): 54% spend 1 to 3 hours a week making social content ([HJI](https://hji.co.uk/small-business-owners-creating-social-media-content)).
- **Where to sell it:** Fiverr and Upwork. Later, direct monthly clients.
- **Typical prices seen:** Fiverr Canva-post gigs mostly list "up to $50", with agency-style gigs at "$50-$100". One gig has 514 reviews at 4.8 stars, another has 40 reviews at 5.0, and one has 18 reviews at 3.7. ([sara_graphics7](https://fiverr.com/sara_graphics7/design-5-editable-social-media-posts-and-canva-templates), [mijalzagier](https://fiverr.com/mijalzagier/design-social-media-template-posts-and-covers), [marijancusin](https://fiverr.com/marijancusin/design-pro-canva-social-media-templates), [designs_media](https://fiverr.com/designs_media/design-20-creative-social-media-posts-instagram-post-design))
- **How Claude helps:** Claude writes all the captions and the calendar, and designs simple graphics from shapes, lettering and the client's own logo and photos. A person reviews everything and exports the image files.
- **Votes:**
  - **Demand: approve.** One gig has more than 500 reviews, and several surveys show owners are short of time for this work.
  - **Safety: approve.** The service uses only original designs and the client's own photos or properly licensed stock. That fits Fiverr's AI rules, provided nothing is mass-produced or reused between clients.
  - **Profit: approve.** The text is fast for Claude, and simple graphics can be drawn as vector files. At the $50-$100 prices seen, the business keeps $40 to $80 after Fiverr's 20%. One caveat: many buyers expect editable Canva files. Claude can't work inside Canva, so the first gig should promise image files plus captions, not Canva templates.

### 3. YouTube channel art (banner plus profile logo)

- **What it is:** A 2560x1440 channel banner that reads well on phone, TV and desktop, plus a matching round profile logo.
- **Who buys it:** New and small YouTube channels, and streamers who also want Twitch and X headers.
- **Where to sell it:** Fiverr and Upwork.
- **Typical prices seen:** Most gigs list "up to $50". Other prices seen were a flat $50 (4 days), $50-$100 (6 days), and "$5" for a logo. One channel-art gig has 1,763 reviews at 4.7 stars, and another has 13 reviews at 4.8. ([cynagg](https://fiverr.com/cynagg/design-exclusive-youtube-channel-art), [jartwork](https://www.fiverr.com/jartwork), [creationsinkgfx](https://www.fiverr.com/creationsinkgfx/create-an-amazing-youtube-avatar), [designtone0](https://fiverr.com/designtone0/design-youtube-banner-or-channel-art))
- **How Claude helps:** Banners and logos are mostly lettering, shapes and colour, which Claude can draw as vector files that scale cleanly. A person checks the files and exports them.
- **Votes:**
  - **Demand: approve.** A single gig has more than 1,700 reviews, which shows steady paid demand.
  - **Safety: approve.** The designs are original, but they must not resemble any brand, game or character logo, and the client must own any photo or mascot they supply.
  - **Profit: approve.** At the $50 or $50-$100 prices seen, the business keeps $40 to $80 after Fiverr's 20%. Delivery is quick once there's a reusable layout. This is the least proven of the three, because Claude's illustration quality hasn't been tested against the competition yet.

## 3. Denied ideas

### 4. YouTube thumbnails (made with the creator's own photos, or for faceless channels)
- **Demand: approve.** This has the strongest demand seen. Fiverr gigs show 8,888, 3,986 and 598 reviews ([search snapshot](https://fiverr.com/eniola_favor/design-3-custom-thumbnails-that-boost-views), [thumbgod](https://www.fiverr.com/thumbgod)), and there are many Upwork job posts.
- **Safety: approve.** It's allowed if the only faces used are the client's own (with permission) and the thumbnail truthfully matches the video, in line with YouTube's rules on misleading AI likenesses ([YouTube AI](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/ai/)).
- **Profit: deny.** Good thumbnails need photo cut-outs and dramatic photo editing, which Claude can't do alone. The market price is low: one guide puts the average Fiverr gig around $18, and $5 gigs exist ([AI Video Bootcamp](https://aivideobootcamp.com/blog/how-to-make-money-ai-thumbnails/)). It isn't proven that the business could deliver competitive quality quickly.

### 5. Thumbnails featuring celebrities or other creators' faces (reaction and commentary channels)
- **Demand: approve.** Commentary-style thumbnail gigs are common in the same listings.
- **Safety: deny.** It uses other people's photos and likenesses without their permission, and YouTube is expanding likeness detection and removal requests ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/09/16/youtube-ai-likeness-detection-deepfakes)).
- **Profit: deny.** It has the same image-editing problem as idea 4, plus the risk of takedowns and refunds.

### 6. Illustrated pet portraits from the customer's photo (digital file, or printed on a shirt or mug)
- **Demand: approve.** Fiverr pet-cartoon gigs show 2,041, 1,598 and 1,214 reviews ([search snapshot](https://fiverr.com/abrehman314/do-custom-vector-cartoon-pet-portrait-dog-cat-portrait)), and Etsy has a large number of these listings ([Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/ca-fr/market/custom_pet_potrait)).
- **Safety: approve.** It's allowed if the customer owns the photo, the style is original, and AI use is disclosed. Etsy's own example of allowed AI work is "a custom portrait of a buyer's pet generated using AI tools", listed as "Designed by" and not called handmade ([FindSkill summary of Etsy's October 2026 rules](https://findskill.ai/blog/etsy-ai-rules-october-2026-seller-guide/)).
- **Profit: deny.** Buyers judge these on whether the drawing looks like their own pet. Claude can't reliably draw that likeness without an image tool, and AI portrait sellers have already pushed digital prices down to about $5-$25 ([Etsy search snapshot](https://etsy.com/listing/1274715432/cartoon-pet-portrait-custom-pet-portrait), [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/)). This could be re-judged after a quality test.

### 7. Pet photo plus name, with an original frame or lettering, printed on a mug or shirt
- **Demand: deny.** Shops do sell this (one Etsy pet mug was about $29 on sale), but I saw no order or review counts. Free do-it-yourself tools at Vistaprint, Sticker Mule, Fourthwall and Chewy do the same job ([Vistaprint](https://www.vistaprint.com/photo-gifts/personalized-mugs/templates/custom/custom-dog-mugs), [Fourthwall](https://fourthwall.com/make-your-own/pet-mugs)).
- **Safety: approve.** It uses the customer's own photo and original lettering. Etsy's October 2026 rule that listings need at least one unedited photo of the real item would mean buying a sample to photograph.
- **Profit: approve.** Rough estimate: $29 sale price, minus about 10% in Etsy fees ([Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees)), minus a Printify mug cost of about $4.63-$7.50, minus US shipping of about $4.55-$5.95 ([PODVector](https://podvector.ai/articles/printify/costs-and-charges/printify-mug-base-cost-full-breakdown-for-pod-sellers)). That leaves about $12-$17 before ads. These are third-party figures from May 2026, and no print-on-demand service has been chosen yet.

### 8. "Disney-style" or movie-character portraits of people or pets
- **Demand: approve.** Gigs selling this exist ([chipchipstudio](https://www.fiverr.com/chipchipstudio/turn-you-prince-princess-disney-character), [pet_house](https://fiverr.com/pet_house/draw-your-pets-into-cute-disney-style)).
- **Safety: deny.** It copies a famous company's characters and style and uses its name. This breaks the business's own rules and marketplace intellectual-property rules.
- **Profit: deny.** Takedown risk makes any profit unreliable.

### 9. Facebook and Instagram ad copy only (no ad management)
- **Demand: deny.** The copy-only gigs I found had few reviews (20 on one, 1 on another) ([seanaherncopy](https://fiverr.com/seanaherncopy/do-facebook-ad-copywriting-and-free-graphic-design)). The large review counts belong to ad-management gigs, not copywriting.
- **Safety: approve.** Original copy is fine as long as it makes no false or exaggerated claims about the client's product.
- **Profit: approve.** Gigs price at $100-$200 with 2-day delivery, and Claude writes copy quickly. Worth re-checking later or bundling into idea 2.

### 10. Facebook and Instagram ad campaign setup and management
- **Demand: approve.** Gigs show 379 and 85 reviews ([search snapshot](https://www.fiverr.com/sinkeedgedesign/plan-setup-and-run-targeted-facebook-advertising-campaign)).
- **Safety: approve.** Running ads for a client is allowed, as long as the ad results promised are honest.
- **Profit: deny.** It is ongoing, hands-on work inside clients' ad accounts with their money, not creative work Claude produces. Reviews show refund-type complaints about wasted ad spend.

### 11. YouTube video scripts
- **Demand: deny.** The gigs I found had thin review counts (31 on one, 1 on another), so there isn't strong evidence of paid demand ([search snapshot](https://fiverr.com/kevimart15/write-a-script-for-your-youtube-video)).
- **Safety: approve.** Original scripts are allowed, but Fiverr's rule against generic AI output and YouTube's rule against mass-produced "inauthentic" content both apply.
- **Profit: approve.** Prices seen were $15 per minute and "up to $50" per script, and Claude writes scripts quickly.

## 4. Gaps in this report

- **YouTube could not be reached.** Every youtube.com page failed with "host not found". The task said youtube.com was allowed, but this session's network didn't allow it. I made no attempt to get around this. No video pages or comments were read, so the "what people ask for" section is thin. If YouTube is meant to be allowed, the session's network settings need it added.
- **Fiverr and Etsy pages could not be opened** for the same reason. All prices and review counts come from search-result snapshots, not live pages, and may be out of date.
- **Instagram:** searches restricted to instagram.com returned no Instagram posts at all. I found no Instagram requests or comments.
- **Platform rules are second-hand.** Etsy's own policy pages weren't reachable. The October 22, 2026 Etsy changes (the unedited-photo rule and AI disclosure) come from seller blogs. Fiverr's rules come from its help-centre pages as they appeared in search results.
- **Survey and fee numbers** mostly come from vendor blogs. I've noted where I couldn't confirm a figure.
- **Claude's image quality is untested.** None of the approved ideas has been tried. The profit votes assume Claude's vector graphics are good enough to sell, which hasn't been proven.
- **Questions I couldn't ask.** The house rules say to ask three questions before a complicated task. This was an owner-approved run started by another agent, so I didn't ask them. These are the three questions whose answers would change the next round:
  1. Will the business use an image-making tool alongside Claude? If so, thumbnails and pet portraits could be re-judged.
  2. Is the owner willing to open a Fiverr or Upwork seller account? All three approved ideas are services sold there.
  3. Which print-on-demand service will be used? That decides the real margin on any mug or shirt idea.

## Next steps (suggested, not decided)

1. Make two or three sample pieces for each approved idea (for example, listing copy for one of the business's own future products) and check them against the competing gigs above.
2. Run a small test: can Claude, alone or with an image tool, make a pet portrait or thumbnail as good as a $25-$50 Fiverr gig? If so, ideas 4 and 6 should go back before the judges.
