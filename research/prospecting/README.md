# Prospecting Hub

Business ideas beyond merchandise: services Claude can help make and sell, such as YouTube thumbnails, ads, or a customer's dog photo turned into a shirt design. Run by the Prospector, whose instructions are in `.claude/agents/prospector.md`.

Reports are named `YYYY-MM-DD-prospecting-report.md`. Every idea goes before the Research Lab's three judges (Demand, Safety, Profit), and only ideas all three approve are recommended.
