## 1. Approved ideas

### 1. Personalized pet-name sweatshirt or hoodie (NEW, no new account needed)

- **What it is:** One of our original cat (or dog) designs, with the buyer's pet name added in our lettering. Examples: "[Name]'s Human" or "Powered by [Name]". The buyer types the name on the Etsy listing, and Printify prints it on the shirt.
- **Who buys it:** Cat and dog owners, and people buying them gifts, especially for Christmas.
- **Where to sell it:** Our Etsy shop, LiquidLav, through Printify. Both accounts already exist.
- **Typical prices seen:** Cat-name and dog-name sweatshirts on Etsy sell for about **$9 to $35**, and embroidered pet sweatshirts for about $21 to $31 (search snapshots, 2026-11-20). Our current sweatshirt price is $37.67 and up, which is above most of them.
- **How Claude helps:** Claude draws the original design and the lettering as vector files, sets the name as a text layer that Printify swaps per order, and writes the title, tags and description. Every order arrives in Printify marked "Review Needed" or "Personalizable", where a quick check catches misspelled names and blocks brand names before printing.
- **Evidence:**
  - FindNiche, a third-party estimate tool, shows a "custom dog sweatshirt, personalized dog ears with name" listing at about 18,256 total sales and about 96 a month, and a custom embroidered name sweatshirt at about 29,878 sales. These are estimates, read only from search snippets ([FindNiche dog](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/custom-dog-sweatshirt-personalized-dog-e-1523211157), [FindNiche name](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/custom-embroidered-sweatshirt-name-on-sl-1485282812)).
  - Many Etsy listings for the same idea, for example a "Custom Cat's Name Sweatshirt" ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1648401909/custom-cats-name-sweatshirt-cats-owner)), and FindNiche calls the niche crowded.
  - Printify calls its Etsy personalization "100% free", with up to 4 text fields per product. Etsy's own personalization box now replaces Printify's old "Personalization Hub" ([Printify](https://printify.com/personalization/), [Printify help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/40720228428433), [Teeinblue guide](https://teeinblue.com/blogs/en/printify-personalization)).
- **Votes** (original, 2026-11-20; the Profit vote is replaced by the Revote 2026-11-21 section at the top):
  - **Demand: approve.** An estimated 18,000-plus sales on one pet-name sweatshirt, and a crowded field of copies, show that people pay for this. But the numbers are third-party estimates.
  - **Safety: approve.** The art is ours and the buyer supplies only a name. AI use must be disclosed, the listing needs one unedited photo of a real sample (Etsy, Oct 22, 2026, [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/)), and orders that ask for brand or character names must be refused.
  - **Profit: approve, narrowly (superseded 2026-11-21).** At our current price ($37.67 and up), CLAUDE.md records about $16 to $23 profit after Etsy fees, and at about $35, the top of the market, roughly $13 to $14 would be left. This is an estimate scaled from those recorded figures, not a new Printify quote. That only holds if the shop doesn't offer free shipping and the sale didn't come through Offsite Ads (see the 2026-11-18 finance lesson). There's one manual step per order, checking the name.

- **Demand: approve.** Many Etsy and UK shops sell this, and there's a FindNiche page for a "custom pet photo and name shirt" ([FindNiche](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/custom-pet-photo-and-name-shirt-personal-4466558752), [Last Night of Freedom](https://www.lastnightoffreedom.co.uk/pets/t-shirts/pet-bootleg/)).

- **Profit: deny.** Tees sell for the low teens to low twenties, every photo needs a person to check it, and free do-it-yourself tools at Fourthwall and Sticker Mule compete ([Fourthwall](https://fourthwall.com/make-your-own/pet-t-shirts)).

**7. Social media post templates sold as Etsy digital downloads**
- **Demand: deny.** Shops sell these ([TheDesignTemplateCo](https://www.etsy.com/shop/TheDesignTemplateCo), [SocialGems](https://etsy.com/shop/SocialGems)), but no order or review counts were visible.
- **Safety: approve.** It's allowed with original designs and AI disclosure.
- **Profit: deny.** Buyers expect editable Canva files, which Claude can't make, and fixed picture files are a weak product. It's also outside the merchandise plan.

**8. YouTube thumbnails (the creator's own photos, or faceless channels)**
- **Demand: approve.** It still has the strongest demand, and creators say thumbnails take 1.5 to 3 hours each.

- **Demand: approve.** These gigs are common.

- **Demand: approve.** Gigs have more than 1,000 reviews.
- **Safety: approve.** It's allowed with the customer's own photo, an original style and AI disclosure.
- **Profit: deny.** Claude can't reliably draw a likeness of the pet, and prices have fallen to $5 to $25.

**11. Pet photo plus name on a mug or shirt (plain layout)**
- **Demand: deny.** No order counts were found, and free do-it-yourself tools exist.
- **Safety: approve.** It uses the customer's own photo and original lettering.
- **Profit: approve.** About $12 to $17 left on a $29 mug (third-party cost figures).

**12. "Disney-style" character portraits**
- **Demand: approve.** These gigs exist.
- **Safety: deny.** It copies a famous company's characters, style and name.
- **Profit: deny.** Takedown risk makes any profit unreliable.

**13. Facebook and Instagram ad copy only**
- **Demand: deny.** The gigs found had few reviews.
- **Safety: approve.** It's allowed with honest claims.
- **Profit: approve.** Gigs charge $100 to $200, and the copy is quick to write.

**14. Ad campaign setup and management**
- **Demand: approve.** Gigs have 379 and 85 reviews.
- **Safety: approve.** It's allowed if the promised results are honest.
- **Profit: deny.** It's ongoing work inside clients' ad accounts, spending their money, not creative work.

**15. YouTube video scripts**
- **Demand: deny.** Review counts are thin.
- **Safety: approve.** It's allowed if the scripts are original and not mass-produced.
- **Profit: approve.** Scripts are quick to write at the prices seen.
