## How to post (owner, by hand)

1. On pinterest.com, click **+** (Create) at the top left, then **Create Pin**.
2. Upload the picture named in the pin.
3. Paste the title, the description and the Etsy link into the matching boxes.
4. Pick or create a board (for example "Christmas Gift Ideas" or "Cozy Winter Reading"), then click **Publish**.
5. Post one or two pins a day rather than all at once.
