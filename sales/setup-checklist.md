# Store setup checklist

The steps to open the Etsy shop and the Amazon storefront. The owner has to do these personally: both sites need your legal name, ID, bank account and tax details. Never save passwords, bank numbers or ID numbers in this repository.

Fees and rules change, so check the numbers on each site's own pages when you sign up.

## Before you start (have these ready)

- [ ] A business email address used only for the stores
- [ ] A shop name (check it's free on both Etsy and Amazon, and isn't someone's trademark)
- [ ] A photo ID (driver's license or passport)
- [ ] A bank account for payouts, and a debit or credit card for fees
- [ ] Your tax number: your Social Security number as a sole owner, or a free EIN from irs.gov if you'd rather not share your SSN
- [ ] A phone number for verification codes

## 1. Print-on-demand service (makes and ships the products)

You don't hold any stock. When someone orders, the print-on-demand service prints the design on the product and ships it.

- [ ] Pick one: **Printify** or **Printful** (both free to join; you pay for each product only when it sells)
- [ ] Sign up, and connect it to your Etsy shop once that exists (step 2)

## 2. Etsy shop

1. [ ] Go to **etsy.com/sell** and tap **Get started**.
2. [ ] Create an account with the business email.
3. [ ] Choose your shop language, country and currency (US dollars).
4. [ ] Enter the shop name.
5. [ ] Skip adding a listing for now if it asks. Listings come from the print-on-demand service later.
6. [ ] Set up payments: enter your bank account and tax details.
7. [ ] Add a card for Etsy's fees, and verify your identity if it asks.
8. [ ] Open the shop, then connect Printify or Printful under its **Sales channels / Stores** page.

Typical Etsy costs: $0.20 to list each item, plus a fee on each sale (about 6.5%) and a payment processing fee. Some countries also have a small one-time setup fee.

Etsy has rules about how items are made. Read its **Creativity Standards** page; designs made with AI help may need to say so in the listing.

## 3. Amazon

There are two ways in. **Start with Merch on Demand.** It fits this business best.

### Merch on Demand (recommended first)

Amazon prints and ships your designs on shirts, hoodies, mugs and more, and pays you a royalty on each sale. It's free to join.

1. [ ] Go to **merch.amazon.com** and tap **Sign up**.
2. [ ] Sign in with an Amazon account (making one just for the business is best).
3. [ ] Fill in the application: business details, tax interview, and bank account for royalties.
4. [ ] Wait for approval. It can take days to weeks, so apply early.
5. [ ] Once you're approved, you start with a small number of designs you can upload, and the limit grows as designs sell.

### Amazon Seller Central (later, optional)

A full Amazon store. Needed only if you want to sell more than Merch on Demand covers.

- Individual plan: about $0.99 per item sold. Professional plan: about $39.99 a month.
- It needs ID and bank checks, and sometimes a short video call to verify your identity.

## When it's done

Tell Claude which steps are finished, but never the passwords or account numbers. The Sales Dock on the station map will light up, and the Seller robot can start preparing listings: titles, descriptions, tags and prices. You still press **Publish** yourself.
