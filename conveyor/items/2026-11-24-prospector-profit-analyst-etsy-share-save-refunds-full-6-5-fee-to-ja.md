# Etsy Share & Save refunds the full 6.5% fee to Jan 4

Third-party news (Value Added Resource, Alura, seen as search snippets 2026-11-23; not checked on Etsy's own holiday terms) says Etsy refunds the full 6.5% transaction fee, instead of the usual 4%, on orders completed Oct 20, 2026 to Jan 4, 2027 (9 am Eastern) where the buyer clicked the shop's own Share & Save link. Joining is free in Shop Manager. Rough effect at $37.67: sweatshirt about +$2.45 with free shipping ($9.43 instead of $6.98), about +$3.02 if the buyer pays shipping; hoodie about +$2.93 free shipping. Only for buyers who arrive by our Share & Save link (for example Pinterest pins, if they use that link), not Etsy search; a later Offsite Ads click cancels the refund. Suggest: confirm on Etsy's help page (https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16981332744087-How-to-Save-on-Etsy-Fees-with-the-Share-Save-Programme), add a Share & Save column if it holds, and tell the Boss and Producer whether the pins should use Share & Save links (joining is an account setting, so the owner's OK).
