# Pet-name sweatshirt: the one service-style idea that needs no new account

All three judges approved a personalized pet-name sweatshirt or hoodie: our original cat design plus the buyer's pet name, using Etsy's personalization box and Printify's free personalization. It runs on the Etsy shop and Printify account we already have. Evidence is weak to moderate (FindNiche estimates of about 18,000 sales on one pet-name sweatshirt, read from search snippets). Profit is about $13 to $16 at $35 to $38 only if the buyer pays shipping and no Offsite Ads fee applies. It needs the owner's OK for a new listing, a sample to photograph (Etsy's Oct 22 rule), and a name check on each order. Fiverr/Upwork ideas are unchanged and stay on hold until January.
