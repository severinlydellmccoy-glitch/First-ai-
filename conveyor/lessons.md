# Lessons learned

Short, practical lessons from real work. Newest first. Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD (agent): lesson`.

- 2026-11-26 (finance-chief): When a price drop is traced to a margin setting (cost ÷ 0.8 instead of ÷ 0.6), "restore the price" must include setting the margin back in Printify, or the same reset can undo the restore; say both in one owner step.
- 2026-11-26 (analyst): Before running the Problem Solver on the "open 2 or more days" rule, read what each old item is waiting for. If every one needs the owner's own words ("publish", "restore", "hide") and there's no `problem` item, skip it until its 3rd day; running it only re-reads blocked work.
- 2026-11-25 (underboss): When an approval says "keep a design live", check which products it covers against `profitability.md`: the book designs' sweatshirt and hoodie pass, but the same design's tote and candle are thin or losing at their current prices, so name the products in the approval.
- 2026-11-25 (scout): Etsy search summaries show huge "review" numbers next to listings (24,000 to 39,000 on Valentine cat sweatshirts). Those are the shop's total reviews, not sales of that listing, so mark them weak. Also check the Amazon Best Sellers snapshot's season before using it: on Nov 25 it was still Halloween, so it was weeks old. For a holiday months away (Valentine's), expect no purchase counts yet, and set a recheck date instead of approving on listings alone.
- 2026-11-25 (problem-solver): When a Printify price moves with no updated_at change, divide the cost by the price: the candle, tote and sticker went from exactly cost/0.6 (40% margin) to cost/0.8 (20% margin), which shows a margin-based reset inside Printify rather than a sale or one of our scripts (none of them sends prices). Bracket the change between two dated profit-table reads in git history.
- 2026-11-25 (analyst): When the shop is waiting on the owner (5 drafts unpublished) and the Christmas cut-off is under 2 weeks away, point the Scout's calendar-triggered run past Christmas (January, Valentine's Day). New Christmas art couldn't be listed in time, so it would only add to the queue.
- 2026-11-24 (boss): When an owner deadline passes unanswered, merge every waiting ask of the same kind into one sentence the owner can copy (here, one "publish" for the 5 drafts plus black/navy cat). Count drafts and colors separately in that sentence: I first wrote "7 drafts" when there were 5 drafts and 2 colors on a live listing.
- 2026-11-24 (finance-chief): When a price may carry an unconfirmed fee (the $0.40 surcharge), set ad stop limits on the lower figure ($3.29 / $4.76, not $3.49 / $4.96); being slightly too careful costs nothing, and a price that moves with no known cause gets "check Etsy, then restore or hide", never ads.
- 2026-11-24 (producer): A hoodie title made by swapping "Sweatshirt" for "Hooded Sweatshirt" can go over 140 characters, so recount both titles with the parser after every word swap (the winter cat hoodie came out at 141). Check the mockup for colors that change per shirt (the winter cat's text is mustard on dark shirts, teal and navy on light) before naming colors in a description.
- 2026-11-24 (profit-analyst): Compare every price with the last profit table, not only costs: Printify's API showed the candle, tote and sticker 25% cheaper overnight with no updated_at change and no logged edit. `profitability.py` now lists price changes since its last read and keeps unconfirmed fees (the $0.40 surcharge, Share & Save) in their own section, out of the profit columns.
- 2026-11-24 (problem-solver): The print provider is on every product in the Printify API (`print_provider_id`, then `/catalog/blueprints/{id}/print_providers.json` for its name): ours is Printify Choice (99) for all apparel. Printify Choice routes each order to a partner printer, so its holiday date can't be inferred from any one printer; only a Printify page confirms it, and printify.com is blocked from here, so ask the owner to read it off the Holiday Hub.
- 2026-11-24 (artist): `mockup.js` now draws hoodies too (`--hoodie`, with `--only=PREFIX` for one design); render into an empty scratch folder and copy only the `*-preview.png` files to `media/final/mockups/`, because the script also writes a contact sheet of everything in its output folder. Keep hoodie drawstrings short so they never touch the top line of text.
- 2026-11-24 (analyst): When an owner deadline in `owner-todo.md` passes with no answer, put an item on the belt for the Boss that same night, so the list never shows the owner a date that's already gone. Order the run so the Boss goes last on nights when the finance team or Problem Solver may change its dates.
- 2026-11-23 (boss): Write a hold rule as a grade line ("go ahead at medium leaning weak or better, hold at weak") plus what the idea may spend while unproven (time only, no sample or ads until a cat sells), so the next Boss can decide without reinterpreting it. And never move an ad stop date on an unverified source: the Dec 8 cut-off waits for our own print provider's date.
- 2026-11-23 (underboss): When a recheck lowers an idea's evidence, set the new grade against the Boss's own hold condition (here, "on hold if the recheck is weak") and say clearly which side it falls on. A grade like "medium leaning weak" can quietly slip past a condition that was meant to stop it.
- 2026-11-23 (scout): Recheck any single high badge before an idea's art is finished. The everyday cat's 700+ came back as 100+ two days later, and the search summary didn't say which variant of the same seller's listing it meant. Approve a theme only on several named listings agreeing, never on one badge. For dates like the Christmas cut-off, search for third-party roundups of Printify's per-provider deadlines (printify.com can't be opened here). They gave US Standard Dec 8 and also turned up the $0.40 US holiday surcharge.
- 2026-11-23 (artist): For a "no Christmas" design, pick colors as well as symbols: the shared light-shirt red (`main`) plus green reads as Christmas, so the winter cat uses navy and teal text (`winter`, `winterAccent` in WAYS) and a knit hat instead of anything Santa-like. Also check that a hood or hat fully covers the cat's ears; half-hidden ear tips looked like a mistake.
- 2026-11-23 (prospector): Search for time-limited Etsy fee promotions on every short check, not just fee hikes: a "fee change" search turned up Share & Save refunding the full 6.5% through Jan 4, 2027 (about +$2.45 on a free-shipping sweatshirt from our own links), which no file in the repo mentioned.
- 2026-11-23 (problem-solver): A draft's design key must not be the start of another design's name: `create_drafts.py` skips a product when any front file starts with the key, so a key like `book-lover` would wrongly match the live `book-lover-snowed-in` products. Check the dry run says "copy of" and not "already in Printify" before `--apply`.
- 2026-11-23 (analyst): Count products in `check_listings.py` against every drafts deadline: it shows at a glance whether a draft really exists in Printify (book design 2 had finished art and copy but no drafts two days before its due date). Also, finished jobs can leave their conveyor items open (the dachshund drafts), so check the work, not just the item status, before calling something overdue.
- 2026-11-22 (boss): Check `art/final/` and `media/final/` against every deadline in the last decisions each run: the second book design and its copy landed days early, so its draft and owner dates could move up (Nov 29 to Nov 25, Dec 1 to Nov 29) and gain Cyber Monday.
- 2026-11-22 (underboss): Compare each vote's wording with the report's own caveats: a Demand vote can call a badge "real" that the same report says couldn't be tied to one product. Also compare estimates such as the Christmas cut-off date with the previous round, since they can move without a source (Dec 10 became Dec 1).

- 2026-11-22 (scout): Amazon search summaries often show a "bought in past month" badge without tying it to one product (a 900+ on the hot cocoa page belonged to nobody in particular). Only count a badge that's named with its listing. Search for the brand name in the snippet (amazon.com only) to confirm it, and mark the rest weak.
- 2026-11-22 (problem-solver): FMP's `historical-price-eod/full` prices are not dividend-adjusted; `historical-price-eod/dividend-adjusted` (fields `adjClose`, same volume) is in the free plan at the same cost of one request per ticker. `daily_screen.py` now uses it. Market holidays live in `tools/market/market_calendar.py` (through 2027); check it before any stock run.
- 2026-11-22 (producer): Look at the mockup before describing the art, not only the hand-off text: the Artist's note said "a folded plaid blanket", but the picture shows a stack of three. Write length placeholders in the file, then fill them from the parser so the counts in the headings are never typed by hand.
- 2026-11-22 (finance-chief): Price a sample from the profit table (Printify cost plus Standard shipping: a sweatshirt is about $26.66 before tax), not from an old round figure; the "$20 a sample" note hid that two samples plus a week of ads go over the $50 cap.
- 2026-11-22 (artist): Check designs at shirt size on every sale color before finishing: dark-ink steam lines vanished on black and navy, and pale-blue snowflakes faded on sport grey. Give such details a per-colorway color (`steam`, `snowDeep` in WAYS in build.js).
- 2026-11-22 (profit-analyst): Put every fee case in the profit table as its own finished column (for example profit with Offsite Ads, per shipping setting) instead of a separate fee column; agents subtracted a buyer-pays fee from the free-shipping profit and got the wrong answer.
- 2026-11-22 (finance-operator): Printify has two shops (29819127 Etsy, 29819029 disconnected); check orders in both, so an order on the wrong shop isn't missed. Ads at $3 a day (about $21) plus $40 of samples goes over the $50 weekly cap, so schedule them in different weeks.
- 2026-11-22 (analyst): The cycle runs at 9:17 pm Central, which is already the next day in UTC, so `conveyor.py` stamps items (and file names) one day ahead of the Central date. When judging "open 2 or more days", subtract a day from the item's `created:` date, or count from the dispatch that created it.
- 2026-11-21 (boss): Other rooms work during the same night's run. Re-check `media/final/`, `art/final/` and item statuses just before writing hand-offs; the Producer had already finished the dachshund copy I was about to re-assign. And hold a profit-passing idea if a step that can't be rushed (a mailed sample, a hand check per order) misses the deadline.
- 2026-11-21 (producer): When swapping a pin picture, also rewrite the file's "About the pictures" note; a sed on the file name alone leaves stale advice that contradicts the pins.
- 2026-11-22 (artist): Playwright PNGs carry no DPI tag (earlier finals had none). Stamp 300 DPI with Pillow (`save(f, dpi=(300, 300))`) after rendering, and draw characters as a thick ink silhouette under a coat-colored one so overlapping body parts get one clean outline.
- 2026-11-21 (underboss): The Offsite Ads fee in `profitability.md` is 15% of price plus shipping, so it only fits the buyer-pays-shipping case. With free shipping, take 15% of the price alone (sweatshirt $6.98 - $5.65 = about $1.33).
- 2026-11-21 (prospector): For any price-based vote, work out the lowest price that still clears the $6 floor with free shipping (sweatshirt: price x 0.905 - $27.11; about $36.60) and state it, so a later price cut can be checked without a new vote.
- 2026-11-20 (boss): Before picking new art, look in `art/drafts/` first. A second bookish design ("Blanket. Cocoa. chapter twelve.") was already drafted, so it can come back cheaply once the urgent new design is done. Also, this run had no shell: close conveyor items by setting `status: done`, adding `closed:` and appending `**Result:**`, exactly as `tools/conveyor.py close` does.
- 2026-11-20 (underboss): Take every profit vote from the current `finance/profitability.md`, never from the summary figures in CLAUDE.md, which go stale when a cost changes. If a vote quotes a different price, work out both shipping cases at that price (at $35 a free-shipping sweatshirt falls below $6).
